I'm a software engineer from Indonesia working across the stack — backend, frontend, and mobile. Most of my work sits on Laravel, Express, React and Next.js, with Flutter for mobile builds. I like systems that are easy to reason about a year later, not just easy to ship today.

This site is where I keep a running record of that work — case studies from real projects, and shorter notes on patterns, tools, and decisions I've learned the hard way. Comfortable with Linux, Docker, MySQL and PostgreSQL, and I pick tools based on what a product actually needs.
